Our Mission

Hearth & Heritage is dedicated to nurturing the cultural landscapes that define local identity. We focus on preserving historic buildings, oral histories, intangible traditions, and public art while supporting inclusive access and education. Our guiding principles are care, collaboration, and continuity.

Community-Led

Projects begin with local knowledge and respect for living traditions.

Sustainable Care

Conservation plans prioritize low-impact, long-term solutions.

Open Learning

Education programs make history accessible to all ages.

Inclusive Stewardship

We amplify diverse voices and respectful dialogue in decision-making.

History & Timeline

A brief overview of milestones and initiatives that shaped our approach.

  1. 2004

    Founding and First Restoration

    Established by local historians and craftspeople to restore a cluster of 19th-century civic buildings.

  2. 2010

    Oral History Program Launch

    Launched a community interview series to record stories from elders and practitioners.

  3. 2018

    Cultural Mapping Initiative

    Partnered with universities to map intangible cultural assets and community networks.

  4. 2023

    Public Education & Digital Archive

    Published an open-access archive and a school curriculum for local history.

Get Involved

Volunteers are the heart of our work. Whether you can spare a few hours or offer professional skills, there is a place for you.

  • Oral history interviewers: training provided for respectful listening and recording.
  • Event hosts & educators: welcome visitors and lead small groups.
  • Cataloguers & digitizers: help curate our digital archive.
